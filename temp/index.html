📌 Important Instructions for Winter 2026 Exam
  • The Exam Day & Date wise FINAL Time Table for Winter 2026 Theory Examination is displayed on MSBTE portal for the information of students, institutes and all concerned.
  • The duration of the Winter 2026 Theory Examination is 18 days.
  • All students belonging to old schemes shall note that the theory examination shall be as per the paper codes which are offered as equivalent to the courses in old schemes and indicated on their hall ticket.
  • Examination of different semesters/years scheduled on the same day but in different slots shall not be considered as a clash.
